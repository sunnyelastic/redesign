Platform, explained

Full-stack observability means collecting logs, metrics, and traces from every layer of your environment — infrastructure, applications, and user experience — and correlating them in one place so you can answer any question about system behaviour without switching tools.

Agentic observability puts AI agents in the investigation loop. Rather than presenting dashboards for a human to interpret, agents reason over your telemetry, surface probable root cause, and can execute remediation workflows with full transparency.

Faster mean time to resolution, less alert fatigue, and lower cost. AI narrows millions of signals down to the handful that matter, drafts the investigation narrative, and proposes the fix — so engineers spend their time deciding rather than digging.

Cost and openness. Elastic's storage architecture — LogsDB index mode, synthetic _source, and compression — delivers substantially lower storage bills, and the platform is OpenTelemetry-native rather than tied to proprietary agents.

Predictable pricing at scale and a single platform for search, observability, and security. Teams keep full-fidelity data instead of sampling it away to control spend, and can run anywhere — any cloud, on-premises, or air-gapped.

Monitoring tells you whether a known condition is true; observability lets you ask new questions of your data after the fact. Monitoring answers “is it up?” — observability answers “why is it behaving like this?”
